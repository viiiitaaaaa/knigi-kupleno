Deep research. Book: {BOOK}.
Create EXACTLY 30 realistic situations in which a Russian-speaking woman, a solo entrepreneur (sole proprietor), would message her AI assistant. Cover the WHOLE book: every key technique at least once, plus edge cases, traps, and at least 6 situations where the book gives no answer (the right reply is «в книге этого нет»). Base it on the book's actual text and reliable summaries; mark anything you cannot confirm as "not verified". No tables; numbered list 1–30.
