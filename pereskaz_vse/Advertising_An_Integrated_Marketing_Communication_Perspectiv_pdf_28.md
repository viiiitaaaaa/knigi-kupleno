#### Pretesting Finished Ads / Предтестирование готовой рекламы *(стр. 693‑696)*  

**Print pretesting methods** – (стр. 693‑694)  

1. **Portfolio tests** – (стр. 693)  
   *Laboratory method exposing respondents to a mix of control and test ads; measure recall.*  

   **Weaknesses** – (стр. 694)  
   1. Factors other than creativity (product interest, test awareness) may affect recall.  
   2. Recall may not be the best test for low‑involvement products; recognition might be better.  

2. **Readability tests** – (стр. 694)  
   *Uses the **Flesch formula** (Rudolf Flesch) to assess average syllables per 100 words; also considers sentence length, word familiarity, and matches to target audience education.*  

   **Key finding:** *Copy is best understood when sentences are short, words concrete/familiar, and personal references used.*  

   **Limitation:** *Tests format, not content; does not address creativity.*  

3. **Dummy advertising vehicles** – (стр. 695)  
   *Ads placed in “dummy” magazines that mimic normal editorial content; distributed to random homes; respondents read as usual, then are interviewed.*  

   **Advantages:** *More natural setting than portfolio test; reading occurs at home.*  

   **Disadvantages:** *Testing effect remains; product interest may still bias results.*  

**Digital pretesting methods** – (стр. 696‑698)  

1. **Theatre tests** – (стр. 696‑698)  
   *Laboratory setting where participants view pilots of TV programs/commercials; may be in a real theatre or simulated environment.*  

   **Typical procedure** – (стр. 696)  
   - Invite participants (phone, mall intercept, mail).  
   - Offer draw for gifts; collect demographic data.  
   - Participants view program/commercials, complete product‑preference questionnaire.  
   - May use on‑screen dials/keypads for continuous reactions.  

   **Sample output (Table 12.8)** – (стр. 696) – shows ranking of ads by “Ipsos super score” (e.g., Tide – 92.8, NFL – 92.5, etc.).  

2. **On‑air tests** – (стр. 697)  
   *Insert commercials into actual TV programs; often combined with single‑source ad research.*  

   **Key metric:** *Day‑after recall scores.*  

3. **Physiological measures** – (стр. 698‑699)  

| Measure | Description (ENG) | Описание (RU) |
|---|---|---|
| **Pupil dilation** (pupillometrics) | Measures dilation/constriction of pupils in response to stimuli; indicates arousal/attention. | Измеряет расширение/сужение зрачков в ответ на стимулы; указывает на возбуждение/внимание. |
| **Galvanic skin response (GSR / EDR)** | Measures skin conductance changes due to sweat gland activity after stimulus. | Измеряет проводимость кожи, меняющуюся из‑за потоотделения после стимула. |
| **Eye tracking** | Infra‑red sensor follows eye movement; shows which ad elements attract attention, duration, sequence. | Инфракрасный сенсор отслеживает движение глаз; показывает, какие элементы привлекают внимание, как долго и в какой последовательности. |
| **Brain waves (EEG)** | Electroencephalographic measures of electrical frequencies; used to assess alpha activity and hemispheric lateralisation. | Электроэнцефалографические измерения частот мозга; оценивают альфа‑активность и латерализацию полушарий. |

### Advantages of Digital Media (плюсы цифровых медиа) *(стр. 681‑682)*  

- **Cost:** it was a lot cheaper than using traditional media platforms.  
- **Tracking:** tracking is viable and very simple compared to traditional media, which relies on long‑term measurement tools; social media, on the other hand, is relatively quicker.  
- **Targeting:** it is very specific in terms of isolating who the brand or product wants to involve, i.e. families (traditional media would have to reach out to them through various channels).  
- **Participation:** social media allows one‑to‑one business‑to‑consumer (B2C) interaction, which is practically impossible using traditional media.  
- **Intimacy:** traditional media broadcasts to millions of people at once, robbing it of the specificity and dialogue that can be achieved through social media (although traditional is considered as more ‘real’, especially in ‘real life’—staff dressed in footie jerseys, for example).
