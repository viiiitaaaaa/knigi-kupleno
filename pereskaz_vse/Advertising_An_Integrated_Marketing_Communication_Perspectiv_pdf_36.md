## CASE STUDY 4B – Pollinate Energy: How a Social Enterprise Converted Happy Customers into Successful Salespeople (стр. 851)  
* **Founding & mission** – founded 2012; Australian social enterprise operating in India & Nepal; aims to bring life‑changing, environmentally‑friendly technology to urban slums (India) and rural Nepal (energy poverty). (стр. 851)  
* **Initial product** – affordable solar lighting to replace kerosene lamps (health‑risk free). Later expanded to efficient stoves, water filters, etc.; now expanding product line. (стр. 851)  
* **Impact** – > 110 000 products sold in > 1 000 communities; customers save money, gain non‑polluting, more effective products.  
* **Environmental benefit** – one solar lantern can abate **5.62 t CO₂** per year. (стр. 851)  
* **Payment model** – initial deposit → interest‑free repayments over weeks; trust is critical in each transaction. (стр. 851)

### Reaching Offline Communities with Personal Selling and Community Networking (стр. 852)  
* **Why personal selling?** – Target customers lack internet/e‑media; face‑to‑face is sole communication & distribution method. (стр. 852)  
* **Local workforce** – Recruit & develop salespeople **from within** target communities because English‑speaking outsiders cannot authentically interact; most locals do not speak English. (стр. 852)  
* **Training & employment** – Offer training and meaningful jobs to people who want to help peers. (стр. 852)

### Recognising the Importance of Sales Staff (стр. 853)  
* **Customer media & finance gaps** – No internet, no bank accounts/credit cards → face‑to‑face selling is most effective; thus investing in sales staff is critical. (стр. 853)  
* **Training methods** – Mentorship & team selling; 6‑month training (office + hands‑on field) with experienced “Pollinators” as mentors; focus on sales skills & confidence. (стр. 853)  
* **Recruitment** – Primarily word‑of‑mouth in demographically similar neighborhoods; posters aid awareness & brand visibility. (стр. 853)  
* **Compensation & incentives** – Base salary + commissions rewarding individual product sales & monthly targets. (стр. 853)  
* **Employee benefits (uncommon in these communities)** – Education loans for children, personal loans (need‑based), health insurance for employee & family, pension fund, smartphone with internet (paid by Pollinate), gifts & bonuses during major cultural festivals/holidays. (стр. 853)  

#### Incentives for Customers Who Become Brand Ambassadors (стр. 853)  
* **‘Worker Bees’** – Early‑adopting customers who refer new customers; financially rewarded for connecting Pollinators to new customers; trusted community members act as informal brand ambassadors. (стр. 853)  
* **Customer satisfaction** – > 90 % satisfaction due to well‑trained, motivated Pollinators. (стр. 853)

### The Importance of Knowing Your Customers (стр. 854)  
* **Salesperson responsibilities** – Understand culture, customs, concerns; identify household decision‑makers; tailor sales approach accordingly. (стр. 854)  
* **Personal selling advantage** – Ability to observe, ask questions, and adapt messages in real time. (стр. 854)  
* **Recruitment incentives** – Referral bonus **4 000 ₹** for male recruits; **5 000 ₹** for female recruits (≈ AU$80/ AU$100, Dec 2018) to achieve 50 % female Pollinators by 2019 (actual 37.5 % mid‑2018). (стр. 854)  

#### Sales Leading to Positive Social Impact (стр. 854)  
* **Geographic reach** – > 1 000 Indian communities (Bangalore, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Lucknow, Kanpur, Tumkur) + rural Nepal (Kailali, Chitwan).  
* **Growth** – Products sold grew **500 %** in 2018 alone. (стр. 854)  
* **Financial & health benefits** – Saved **≈ 1.2 bn ₹** (AU$23.47 M) → would have bought ~29 million L kerosene. (стр. 854)  
* **Environmental impact** – Prevented > 65 000 t CO₂ emissions in first six years. (стр. 854)  
* **Brand reputation** – Known for durable, high‑quality products; interest‑free payments; reliable repair service; long warranties; strong trust foundation. (стр. 854)

## APPENDIX – “FROM ‘COMING SOON’ TO ‘COME SOON’—A CAREFULLY CONSTRUCTED TOURISM CAMPAIGN IN DISGUISE” (стр. 856)  

### The Client (стр. 856)  
* **Tourism Australia** – Government body promoting Australia for business & leisure travel; aims to increase tourism expenditure to **AU$115 bn** by 2020; primary markets: China, US, UK. (стр. 856)

### The Challenge (стр. 856)  
* US is 2nd‑largest market: **≈ 718 000 visitors**, **US$3.7 bn** spend annually.  
* US share stagnant at **1.3 %** despite “desire” (Australia ranked 3rd globally for holiday desire).  
* Barrier: “tyranny of distance”; competition from Europe & Caribbean (closer). (стр. 856)

### Target Audience (стр. 856)  
* **High‑Value Travellers (HVTs)** – Long‑haul regular travelers; willing to stay longer, spend more, visit multiple regions. (стр. 856)

### Research Insights (стр. 857)  
* Distance not only barrier; **uniqueness & fashionability** of destination also crucial.  
* US outbound spend to Asia ↑ 9.2 % (2017); destinations like Japan viewed as “fashionable”. (стр. 857)  

### Communication Objectives (стр. 857)  
1. **Increase consideration & bookings** among US HVTs.  
2. **Ensure audience identifies one or more Australian strengths** (food & wine, aquatic & coastal, nature & wildlife) as important for destination selection.  
3. **Target ‘high value’ travelers** – willing to stay longer, spend more, visit multiple regions. (стр. 857)

### Media Strategy (стр. 858)  
* Generate **earned media & WOM** with shareable content; compensate for limited budget. (стр. 858)  
* Create **affinity & desire** for Australia by showcasing exclusive experiences; prove rather than claim “Nothing Like Australia”. (стр. 858)  
* Drive **urgency** with lower‑funnel, conversion‑driving assets on Australia.com. (стр. 858)

### Creative Strategy (стр. 859‑860)  
* Existing tagline “There’s Nothing Like Australia” used generic imagery → “category wallpaper”. (стр. 859)  
* **Big Idea:** Pair Australia’s unique nature with unique Australians – iconic personalities (Chris Hemsworth, Steve Irwin, etc.). (стр. 860)  
* **Iconic reference:** “Crocodile Dundee” – 1986 film that acted as a tourism campaign; leverage its cultural cachet. (стр. 860‑861)  

### The Big Idea – “Dundee: The Son of a Legend Returns Home” (стр. 861)  
* **Concept:** A fake movie trailer (no actual film) serving as a tourism campaign disguised as a Hollywood reboot. (стр. 861)  

### IMC Execution – Three Phases (стр. 861‑864)  

#### Phase 1: Tease (стр. 861‑862)  
* **Objective:** Build legitimacy & generate interest before Super Bowl.  
* **Tactics:**  
  * Launch campaign a month before Super Bowl as a typical film – behind‑the‑scenes “leaks”, PR releases, **four blockbuster trailer drops**.  
  * Supporting assets: website, social handles, IMDb/wiki pages, digital, out‑of‑home.  
  * Channels: Online video, TV, social media, banners. (стр. 861)  
* **PR push:** Original Crocodile Dundee (Paul Hogan) appearances; fan‑theories on Reddit & The Weekly Planet podcast. All digital content “ghost‑served” to hide Tourism Australia’s involvement. (стр. 862)  

#### Phase 2: Reveal (стр. 863)  
* **Super Bowl spot:** **60‑second “full trailer”** aired before halftime.  
* **Result:** 82 909 mentions; campaign identified as Tourism Australia; **> 100 million viewers** aware. (стр. 863)  
* **Transition:** Website and assets changed copy from “coming soon” → “come soon” to shift from film to tourism campaign; drive traffic to Australia.com landing page. (стр. 863)  

#### Phase 3: Invite (стр. 863‑864)  
* **Goal:** Convert awareness into bookings.  
* **Tactics:** Retarget engaged HVTs (from Phases 1‑2) with **Australia itinerary‑planning content**.  
* **Content series:** “Why Australia?” – showcases experiences across product pillars; coordinated with airline & OTA partners for tailored offers & banners. (стр. 864)  

### Results (стр. 864‑865)  
* **Intent‑to‑book** ↑ 83 % → **6:1 ROI** for earned coverage. (стр. 864)  
* **Desirability lift** + 50 % (early brand measures). (стр. 864)  
* **Estimated advertising value (EAV):** **US$85.1 M** from 14 478 media articles. (стр. 864)  
* **Social reach:** ≈ 900 million people; **100 million online views**. (стр. 864)  
* **Top‑of‑funnel:** 1 st most‑viewed Super Bowl ad → generated word‑of‑mouth & earned media. (стр. 865)  
* **Middle‑funnel:** 3.44 M visitors to owned channels (97.5 % new); avg. visit 4.19 min; flight‑search queries for Australia ↑ 35 % week after Super Bowl. (стр. 865)  
* **Lower‑funnel:** Goal to grow US market from **US$3.7 bn → US$6 bn**; tracking shows: desirability + 50 %, consideration + 32 %, intent‑to‑book + 83 %. (стр. 865)

### Agencies Involved (стр. 865‑866)  
* **Universal McCann (UM)** – media planning & buying; “Better Science + Better Art” approach; first to break internal silos, build fully integrated teams. (стр. 865)  
* **Droga5** – independent agency (London & New York); brand‑purpose‑centric communications; clients include Google, Heineken, etc. (стр. 865)
